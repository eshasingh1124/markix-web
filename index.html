Things people ask us.

Is Markix a tool or a service?

A service. There is no dashboard to learn. We run our own models on your data, and you get a clear plan, a call with us and regular follow-ups.

What do you need from me?

Two minutes. Add us as a read-only viewer on your store and ad accounts, or share a read-only token. We pull everything ourselves. If you have a list of past sales and launches, that helps.

How is this different from my Meta or Google dashboard?

Each platform counts any sale that touched its ads, so they overlap and over-claim. We start from your total sales and work out what each channel added on top of what you'd have sold anyway.
