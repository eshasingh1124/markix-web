Questions

Is Markix software I have to learn?

No. It's a service. We do the analysis and explain it to you. You just get the answers and the plan.

What is marketing mix modelling?

A way of working out how much each ad channel adds to your total sales, using your sales and spend history. It doesn't rely on clicks or cookies, so it isn't fooled by platforms claiming the same sale.

Can you change my ads or see customer details?

No. Access is read-only, and we only use totals like daily sales and spend. We never touch your campaigns.

How soon will I see results?

A first read of your data in week one, your full report and plan in week two, and a city test result within about six weeks.
